Part 2: Reading & Translation (22-31)

Read the following passage and answer the questions:

Have you ever wondered why hanging out with friends makes you feel so good? Research shows that socializing has incredible benefits for both our physical and mental health. When we spend time with others, our bodies release hormones that reduce stress and boost our mood. Regular social interaction can actually strengthen our immune system and help us live longer lives. People who maintain strong friendships tend to have lower rates of depression and anxiety. However, quality matters more than quantity. Having a few close friends is more beneficial than knowing many people superficially.

Part 3: Written Questions (32-42)

32. Rewrite using reported speech: "Where are you going?" she asked me.

She asked me where I was going.

33. Rewrite using reported speech: "Did you finish the report?" he asked me.

He asked me if / whether I had finished the report.

34. Rewrite using reported speech: "Why are you crying?" my mother asked me.

My mother asked me why I was crying.

35. What are the benefits of socializing according to the text?

It reduces stress, boosts mood, strengthens the immune system, and helps us live longer.

36. Mention two challenges of making plans with friends.

Packed schedules, preferring online interaction, too many entertainment options, or changing friend groups.

37. What is the purpose of a Review?

To inform and to persuade.

38. What are the 4 parts of a Review?

1. Introduction & Overall Impression. 2. Specific Details. 3. Pros and Cons. 4. Conclusion & Recommendation.

39. What is the difference between "pros" and "cons"?

"Pros" are advantages or positive points, while "cons" are disadvantages or negative points.

40. Write a sentence using the idiom "hit the town".

Let's hit the town tonight to celebrate!

41. Write a sentence using the idiom "through thick and thin".

True friends support each other through thick and thin.

42. Write a short recommendation for a restaurant you visited.

Overall, I recommend this restaurant for its delicious food, though it might be better for casual dinners rather than special occasions.
