Part 2: Reading & Translation (22-31)

Read the following passage and answer the questions:

Beneath the surface of the warm, crystal-clear ocean lies a breathtaking world known as the coral reef. Often called a "colorful underwater city," the reef is home to thousands of marine species. These ecosystems are incredibly diverse and provide essential shelter, food, and protection for ocean life. Coral reefs grow slowly over thousands of years, but they are surprisingly fragile. Pollution, climate change, and destructive fishing practices pose serious threats to these natural wonders. When conditions are poor, corals bleach, losing their vivid colors, and becoming lifeless.

35. What is the main idea of the Coral Reefs passage?

Coral reefs are vital ecosystems but they are fragile and face many threats, so we must protect them.

36. Give two ways to protect coral reefs.

Educating communities, promoting eco-friendly tourism, reducing pollution, and banning destructive fishing practices.

37. What are the three main parts of a short story?

40. Define "characters" in a short story.
