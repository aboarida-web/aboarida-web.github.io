Part 3: Written Questions (32-42)

32. Rewrite using reported speech: "I bought a new car yesterday," said Ahmed.

Ahmed said that he had bought a new car the day before.

33. Rewrite using reported speech: "Where do you live?" he asked me.

He asked me where I lived.

34. Rewrite using reported speech: "Don't touch the wires," the technician told us.

The technician told us not to touch the wires.

35. Complete the sentence with a suitable idiom: We need to .......... to prepare for the final exams.

lay the groundwork

36. Complete the sentence with a suitable idiom: If the power goes out, the whole factory will ..........

come to a standstill

37. Write an introduction for an argumentative essay about "The Impact of Technology on Jobs". Include a hook and a thesis statement.

Hook: Have you ever wondered if a robot could take your job? Thesis Statement: I believe that while technology replaces some jobs, it creates new opportunities and improves productivity.

38. What are the three main parts of an argumentative essay?

1. Introduction (hook + thesis statement). 2. Body paragraphs (supporting reasons + opposing view + refutation). 3. Conclusion (restating the claim).

39. Write a sentence to state an opinion using "I believe that..."

I believe that technology makes our lives easier.

40. Write a sentence to introduce an opposing view.

Some people say that machines will replace human workers.

41. What is a "refutation"?

It is an explanation of why the opposing view is weak or wrong.

42. Write a conclusion sentence using "In conclusion...".

In conclusion, technology is a powerful tool that can improve our lives if we use it wisely.
